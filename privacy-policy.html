Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sound Wave Rhythm (accessible via our website). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our interactive audio rhythm visualizers.

2. Microphone Access and Real-Time Audio Processing

Our audio rhythm visualizer requires access to your device's microphone to capture sound and generate the animations.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee: All audio processing is executed completely locally on your device within your web browser using the HTML5 Web Audio API.
  • No audio data is recorded, saved, or stored.
  • No audio data is transmitted, sent, or shared with our servers or any third parties.
  • The moment you stop the visualizer or close the tab, the microphone stream is immediately terminated.

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our site to support its free availability. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Sound Wave Rhythm uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, logins, or registration to use our services. We do not collect or store personal identification information such as your name, email address, IP address, or physical location.
